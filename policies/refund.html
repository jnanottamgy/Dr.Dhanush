Cancelling an order

You can cancel your order on the same day you place it. Write to us at drjhrnd5@gmail.com or call 8431218956 with your order number, and we will refund you in full.

After that day we have already started weighing and packing your order, and it can no longer be cancelled.

Returns

We do not accept returns. Everything we sell is food or household goods, and most of it is weighed out and packed by hand once you order it. Once a pack has left us we cannot take it back or put it back into stock.

Please check the pack size and the quantity on the product page before you order.

If something is wrong with your order

If your order reaches you damaged, or it is not what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery at drjhrnd5@gmail.com or on 8431218956.

Please send photographs of the item, of the pack label, and of the outer parcel it came in. The photographs matter: they are how a damage claim is settled with the courier, and without them there is very little we can do.

We will look into it and tell you what we can do. Any replacement or refund in these cases is at our discretion.

Refunds

Where we do agree a refund, the money goes back to the same method you paid with, within 5 to 7 working days. We do not sell on cash on delivery, so every order has an original payment method to return to. Your bank may take a few days more to show it.

None of this affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
