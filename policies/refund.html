Cancelling an order

You can cancel any order before it is dispatched and you will be refunded in full. Write to us at drjhrnd5@gmail.com or call 8431218956 with your order number. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled, and the returns section below applies instead.

Returns

Everything we sell is food or household goods, and we weigh and pack most of it to order. For food safety we cannot take back a pack that has been opened, and we cannot accept a return simply because you changed your mind.

We will replace or refund an item in any of these cases:

  • the pack arrived damaged, leaking or broken;
  • you were sent the wrong item, or the wrong pack size;
  • the item is past its best before date, or too close to it to be usable, on the day it reaches you;
  • the net quantity in the pack is short of what the pack declares.

How to report a problem

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, at drjhrnd5@gmail.com or on 8431218956. Please send photographs of the item, of the pack label, and of the outer parcel it came in. The photographs matter: they are usually how we settle a damage claim with the courier, and we cannot process a damage claim without them.

Please keep the item and its packaging until the claim is settled.

Refunds

Once we have accepted a return we will refund you within 5 to 7 working days. The money goes back to the same method you paid with — we do not sell on cash on delivery, so every order has an original payment method to return to. Your bank may take a few days more to show it.

Where we send a replacement instead of a refund, we pay the delivery on the replacement. Where a return is our error or the courier's, we pay the return delivery too.

Items we cannot take back

  • Packs that have been opened or used, unless the contents are defective.
  • Items reported to us more than 48 hours after delivery.
  • Items returned without their original packaging and label.

None of this affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
