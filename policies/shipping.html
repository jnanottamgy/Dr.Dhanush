Where we deliver

We deliver anywhere in India. We do not ship outside India at present.

Packed to order

We do not keep sealed stock sitting on a shelf. Most of what we sell is weighed out and packed once you order it, which is why the goods reach you fresh and why dispatch takes a day or two rather than an hour.

Dispatch and delivery time

  • Orders are packed and handed to the courier within 2 to 3 working days.
  • Delivery after that normally takes 3 to 7 working days, depending on where you are.
  • Deliveries to remote pin codes, and deliveries during the monsoon, can take longer. We are in the Western Ghats and heavy rain does occasionally hold up a dispatch. If that happens to your order we will tell you.

We do not dispatch on Sundays or public holidays.

Delivery charges

Delivery is charged by weight and destination. The exact charge is shown at checkout, before you pay. There is no charge added after you have paid.

Tracking

We send you the courier and tracking number by email as soon as your order is dispatched, and on WhatsApp as well if you have given us a number.

If delivery fails

Couriers normally attempt delivery two or three times. If the parcel comes back to us undelivered we will contact you and either send it again, with delivery charged again, or refund you for the goods less the original delivery charge.

Damaged parcels

If a parcel reaches you visibly damaged, photograph it before you open it if you can, and tell us within 48 hours. See our refund policy for what happens next.
