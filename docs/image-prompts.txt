--- A. CATALOGUE SHOT (image 1, every product, white background) ---

Professional e-commerce product photograph. Keep the product and its packaging, label, lettering, logo and colours exactly as in the source image. Do not redesign, re-letter, translate or invent any packaging detail. Re-light only: soft even diffused daylight from the upper left with gentle fill from the right. No harsh glare, no blown highlights, no glossy commercial sheen. Pure white seamless background, hex FFFFFF. Product centred and upright, shot straight on at eye level, filling about 80 percent of the frame with even margin all round. Soft natural contact shadow directly beneath so the product sits on the surface. Colour accurate and true to the original. Sharp focus edge to edge with crisp detail on all label text. Square 1:1 ratio, 2048 by 2048 pixels. Photorealistic, restrained and natural rather than glossy.

NEGATIVE:
text changes, invented labels, redrawn labels, altered branding, watermark, props, hands, people, extra products, studio reflections, oversaturation, HDR, illustration, cartoon


--- B. LIFESTYLE SHOT (image 2, brand palette) ---

Keep the product and its packaging, label, lettering and colours exactly as in the source image. Do not redesign or invent any packaging detail. Place the product on a dark weathered wood surface with a small natural scatter of loose CONTENTS beside it. Background is a soft warm grey paper tone, hex F4F2ED, falling into a gentle blur of deep green shade canopy foliage, hex 1F4034, in the upper corners. A hint of red laterite earth tone, hex A4552F, in the wood. Soft directional morning light from the left, slightly misty and diffused as after monsoon rain. Shallow depth of field. Muted natural colour, cool green rather than warm golden. Photorealistic and unstyled. Square 1:1 ratio, 2048 by 2048 pixels.

NEGATIVE:
text changes, invented labels, redrawn labels, altered branding, watermark, hands, people, extra products, oversaturation, HDR, illustration, cartoon, burlap, hessian sacking, rustic props, coffee cups, warm orange colour grade


--- C. TEXTURE SHOT (image 3, no packaging in frame) ---

Macro photograph of CONTENTS filling the entire frame, shot from directly above on a soft paper toned surface, hex F4F2ED. Natural soft daylight. Shallow depth of field. Rich texture with visible natural variation between individual pieces. Muted cool natural colour grade. No packaging, no text, no props. Photorealistic. Square 1:1 ratio, 2048 by 2048 pixels.

NEGATIVE:
packaging, labels, text, watermark, hands, people, props, oversaturation, HDR, illustration, cartoon


--- D. BACK OF PACK (image 4, the declarations panel) ---

This image has one job: make the printed text readable. Do not let any
tool beautify it. See the warning at the bottom.

Correct and clarify this photograph of printed product packaging. Do not alter, redraw, re-letter, re-space, translate, spell-correct or regenerate any text, number, code or symbol. Every character must remain exactly as photographed, including the FSSAI licence number, batch code, dates, net quantity, MRP and consumer care details. Correct the perspective so the pack face is flat and square to the camera. Remove glare, hotspots and reflections from the plastic film or laminate. Even out uneven lighting across the panel. Increase sharpness and local contrast so small print becomes legible. Reduce noise without smoothing or softening letterforms. Neutral white balance, colour accurate. Crop tight to the pack face with a small even margin. Square 1:1 ratio, 2048 by 2048 pixels. Photorealistic.

NEGATIVE:
redrawn text, regenerated text, altered numbers, corrected spelling, translated text, invented label detail, smoothed lettering, blurred small print, watermark, added graphics, stylised filter, warm colour grade

================================================================
BEFORE ACCEPTING ANY IMAGE
1. Zoom in on the label. Tools garble text - ARABICA becomes ARAB1CA.
1b. On the back-of-pack shot, check EVERY digit against the raw photo.
2. Does the pack match the real pack? If not, reject it.
3. Same framing and light direction across all 30 products.
4. Save as 2048x2048, under 20MB, named to match the catalogue.

PART 2 - BRAND & SITE IMAGES (hero, categories, story bands)
These show place, not product. Nothing here carries a legal
declaration, so generating them is fine.

BUT: prefer the client's OWN estate photographs wherever they
exist. Provenance is the entire selling point of this brand - a
real photograph of their land beats a generated one every time.
Generate only to fill gaps.

And do not generate a claim: no sprawling 500-acre plantation if
they farm twelve acres, no grand estate bungalow that is not
theirs. Atmosphere and place, not specifics they cannot back.
================================================================


--- THE HOUSE STYLE BLOCK ---
Paste this into EVERY prompt in Part 2, then add the subject line.
Consistency across the set matters more than any single image.

Photographed in the Western Ghats of Karnataka during the monsoon season. Cool, damp, misty atmosphere with soft diffused light filtering through a dense forest canopy. Muted natural colour grade built on deep shade green, hex 163A2B, soft pale paper grey, hex F2F0EA, and red laterite earth, hex 9C4A28. Overcast or early morning light, never golden hour, never warm orange. Shot on a full frame camera with a prime lens, shallow to medium depth of field, fine natural grain, no HDR. Restrained, editorial, documentary. Unstyled and unposed.

NEGATIVE (use with all of Part 2):
warm golden hour, orange colour grade, HDR, oversaturation, burlap, hessian sacking, mason jars, latte art, coffee cups, kraft paper, rustic props, stock photo styling, lens flare, vignette, people looking at camera, text, watermark, illustration, cartoon
