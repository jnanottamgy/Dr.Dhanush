## Fixed during this audit

### Alt text on all 70 pack photographs — was 64 missing

**64 of 70 images had an empty `alt` attribute (91%).** The six that had one
repeated the product title verbatim, which is weak: it duplicates the H1 and
describes nothing.

All 70 now carry descriptive alt text, pushed and **verified by reading every
record back**. Source of truth is **`catalogue/image_alt.py`**, so it is in git
and re-pushable, same pattern as `descriptions.py` and `seo.py`.

This matters more on this store than on most. Google Images is a real entry
point for **marati moggu, sikakai, nellikai, antuvala, chekke, sasive** — local
names nobody else competes for — and alt text is one of the few signals an
answer engine has about what a pack photograph shows.

Written under the same discipline as the declarations: **the photographs were
not looked at from here, so no line claims a visual detail.** Each is built from
what the record states — product, maker, pack size from the variant, origin from
the listing. The container is named from the published description, so the
syrups read "700 ml bottle" and Kalpatharu "1.720 kg tin" rather than "pack".

Where a product has more photographs than pack sizes (filter coffee, nice
coffee, Sanjivni, cloves), the extra frames name the product and origin but
**assert no size**, because which pack is in which frame is not something the
record settles. That leaves 67 unique strings across 70 images; the three
repeats are those extra frames, and inventing a difference would be worse.

## Critical — fix first

### 1. No Product structured data anywhere · Schema 15/100

Verified from source, not inferred. There is no `structured_data` filter call
and no `application/ld+json` in `layout/theme.liquid`, `snippets/meta-tags.liquid`,
`snippets/scripts.liquid`, `blocks/_product-details.liquid` or `blocks/price.liquid`,
and **no `structured-data.liquid`, `json-ld.liquid` or `schema.liquid` snippet
exists** in the live theme.

So the store publishes **no Product, Offer, Organization, LocalBusiness or
BreadcrumbList markup**. Consequences, in order of money:

- **No price, availability or rating in Google's result** — the rich snippet that
  makes a shopping result worth clicking.
- **Google Shopping and the AI shopping surfaces read Product/Offer.** Without
  it the catalogue is invisible to them.
- **No Organization or LocalBusiness** means Google has no entity for Malnad
  Products — despite the store already publishing everything one needs: legal
  name, address at Devaramane, two phone numbers, FSSAI 21223055000121, trading
  since 1999.

**This is the single highest-value SEO work left on the store**, and unlike the
items below it does not need anyone's login — it is a theme snippet. It cannot
be written from this container, because **live-theme writes are blocked by the
connector** (established 28 Sep): it needs duplicate → edit → publish, or
Jnanottam in the theme editor.

### 2. The homepage has no title and no meta description

`shop.description` reads **null**. Two effects, not one:

- The homepage falls back to the bare shop name in search.
- `snippets/meta-tags.liquid` sets `og_description` as
  `page_description | default: shop.description | default: shop.name`, so **every
  page without its own description shares `og:description` = "Malnad Products"**.
  That is what WhatsApp, Facebook and X show when the shop is shared — and
  WhatsApp is this business's main channel.

**The wording is already written** — `HOMEPAGE_TITLE` and `HOMEPAGE_DESCRIPTION`
in `catalogue/seo.py`. It cannot be set from the API; it is Online Store →
Preferences, in a browser. **Ten minutes, and it is the cheapest point on the
board.**

## High

### 3. Collection pages have no filters

The free **Shopify Search & Discovery** app is not installed — `appInstallations`
shows three apps and it is not among them. So the nine collection pages offer no
faceted navigation. The quotation promises "search and filters" under item 2, so
this is an unbuilt scope item as much as an SEO one. Free, one install.

### 4. No AI-search groundwork · AI readiness 45/100

No `llms.txt`, no `robots.txt.liquid` in the theme (so Shopify's default applies
and there is no explicit AI-crawler policy), and no Organization entity markup —
which is the same gap as finding 1.

What is already good, and worth saying: the product copy is clean, declarative
and free of marketing padding, which is exactly what gets quoted by an answer
engine. The entity facts are real and consistent. The structural work is missing,
not the substance.

### 5. No analytics and no Search Console

Nothing is measuring anything. No GA4, no Search Console, so there is no
indexation data, no query data and no field CWV. **Until Search Console is
connected, nobody can say whether these 68 pages are indexed at all.**

## Medium

- **Heading hierarchy skips a level.** The product template renders `<h1>` for
  the title and then `<h3>You may also like</h3>` with no `<h2>` between them.
  Read from the live `templates/product.json`.
- **`<meta name="theme-color" content="">`** is emitted empty by
  `meta-tags.liquid`. Should be the brand green `#1F4034`.
- **`og:image` is emitted with an `http:` prefix** alongside the `https:`
  secure_url. Shopify's own default pattern, harmless, dated.
- **Product descriptions are short** — one or two sentences, roughly 30 words.
  They are good sentences and deliberately so, but on a product page that is thin
  by search standards. The declarations panel adds real unique content below them,
  which mitigates it.
- **The `frontpage` collection has null SEO and one product.** Shopify creates it;
  it is not in the Shop menu. Harmless but worth not leaving indexable.

## Verified good — do not "fix" these

- **Canonical tags** on every page, from `meta-tags.liquid`.
- **68 of 68 SEO titles and meta descriptions** — 57 products, 9 collections, both
  pages. No duplicates, no prices, local search terms used where they are the
  search term, titles capped at 42 characters so the ` – Malnad Products` append
  lands inside Google's 60-character display width.
- **All 9 collections carry an image with real alt text.**
- **All 57 products ACTIVE and on the Online Store channel**, verified by
  `resourcePublicationsCount`, which is the check that matters rather than
  `status`.
- **The product template is clean.** H1 on the title, declarations block wired
  in, and — checked specifically — **none of Horizon's default "Free shipping
  over…" / "Free returns" preset blocks are live.** Those strings exist in the
  theme's schema presets, and on a store with weight-based delivery and a paid
  returns policy they would have been a false claim on 57 pages. They are not in
  `templates/product.json`.
- **No clothing placeholder accordions** (Materials / Care instructions / Fit)
  from Horizon's presets either.

## What could not be measured from here, and why

Stated rather than guessed, because a score with invented inputs is worse than a
short one.

| Not measured | Reason |
|---|---|
| Core Web Vitals, LCP, INP, CLS | The session egress proxy answers **403** to `malnadproducts.in` and to `google.com`. No crawler, browser or PageSpeed call can leave this container. |
| Rendered HTML, actual emitted JSON-LD | Same. The schema finding is from **theme source**, which is strong evidence but not the rendered page. **Confirm with Google's Rich Results Test** once a page can be opened. |
| Indexation, impressions, CTR, queries | No Search Console. |
| Organic traffic | No GA4. |
| Backlinks | No Moz, Ahrefs or DataForSEO credentials. |
| Image file weight and format | The API reports dimensions (1190 × 1684 on all 70) but not bytes or whether Shopify is serving WebP. |
