## 4. Health claims printed on the packaging — needs a decision

Three products carry wording that a food label is not permitted to carry:

- **Diabeat decoction** — the label reads *for diabetic patients*
- **Malenadu Special Nellikai powder** — *rich in Vitamin C and cooling agent for both body and eyes*
- **Amla syrup** — badged *HEALTH DRINK*

Under the Drugs & Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act and the FSSAI
advertising rules, a food cannot claim to treat, prevent or manage a condition.
**Reproducing that wording on the website is riskier than printing it on a pack**,
because an online listing is an advertisement.

Recommendation: list the products, keep the brand names as printed, and write
neutral descriptions. Do not repeat the claim. This one is worth ten minutes with
the client rather than a decision made by me.
