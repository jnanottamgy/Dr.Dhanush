02

What has been delivered

  • The shop is live at malnadproducts.in — your own domain, with SSL, in your name.
  • 57 products across 63 pack sizes, in nine sections, each with its own price, its own photograph and its own printed declarations read off the pack.
  • Delivery priced by weight and distance — four zones, seven weight bands, twenty-eight rates, built twice over so a product added later is also covered.
  • Razorpay live and taking payments, with the website registration approved and Magic Checkout running, so Shopify's own 2% does not attach to each order.
  • All six store policies, the About and Contact pages, the navigation and the footer.
  • GST and HSN on every product, and a script that turns a monthly order export into the HSN-wise sheet your accountant files from.
  • An owner's manual, and a plain-English guide to how the money reaches your bank.
  • QR codes for the shop — static, print-ready, with no third party who can switch them off.
