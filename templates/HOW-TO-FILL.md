# Catalogue template — how to fill it

One **row per pack size**. A coffee sold as 250 g, 500 g and 1 kg is three rows
with the same Product Name. Shopify groups them automatically into one product
page with a size selector.

Open in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice. Keep the header row exactly as it is.

## Columns that MUST be filled — legally required

These come from the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules. A product
missing any of them **cannot be published** — the store is configured to refuse
it. Penalties under these rules are assessed per package, so an incomplete
listing across a large consignment is expensive.

| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| `Net Quantity` | With unit, e.g. `250 g`. Must match the pack. |
| `MRP (INR incl. all taxes)` | The maximum retail price, tax inclusive. |
| `Manufacturer or Packer Name` | Legal name of whoever packs it. |
| `Manufacturer or Packer Full Address` | Full postal address with PIN. |
| `Date of Manufacture or Packing` | `DD/MM/YYYY`. |
| `Best Before / Use By` | `DD/MM/YYYY`, or `12 months from packing`. |
| `Country of Origin` | Usually `India`. Tightened for online listings by the Feb 2026 amendment. |
| `Consumer Care Name` / `Phone` / `Email` | A real person and a number that is answered. |
| `FSSAI Licence Number` | Same on every row unless products are packed under different licences. |

## Tax columns

| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| `HSN Code` | Roasted/ground coffee `0901`. Spices `0904`–`0910`. |
| `GST Rate (%)` | `5` for coffee and most whole or ground spices. Value-added blends can be `12`. |

**Have the accountant confirm the rate on every row before we publish.** Getting
this wrong means every invoice is wrong, and invoices cannot be quietly reissued.

## Commercial columns

- `Selling Price (INR)` — what the customer actually pays. May be below MRP.
- `Stock Qty` — units on hand right now, per pack size.
- `Shipping Weight (g)` — packed weight including packaging, not net weight.
- `SKU` — your own code. Any format, must be unique per row.

## Story columns — optional but they sell the product

`Estate / Origin`, `Altitude (ft)`, `Harvest Month`, `Roast Level`,
`Ingredients`, `Storage Instructions`. Provenance is the reason someone pays
estate prices instead of supermarket prices. Fill these in wherever you can.

## Images

`Image File Names` — semicolon-separated, e.g. `mac-1.jpg;mac-2.jpg`. Send the
actual files in a folder alongside this sheet. First image is the main one.

Shoot on a plain background in daylight. Minimum 1600 px on the long edge.
Poor photography is the ceiling on the conversion rate — it matters more than
any other single thing in this sheet.

## When done

Send back the filled sheet **and** the image folder. Do not send passwords,
licence scans or bank details with it — those are not needed here.
