01

What this is, in one paragraph

Your shop is built, live and technically sound. What it does not yet have is a relationship with Google. Nothing is submitted, nothing is measured, and the shop has never been listed on Google Maps. This is the work that puts Malnad Products in front of someone who is searching and does not yet know your name.

This is separate from the search listings already delivered

Worth being plain about, because the two sound alike. The build already included the page titles and descriptions for all 68 pages, the alt text on all 70 pack photographs, and the hidden product data that lets Google show a price. That work is finished and paid for. It is the shop’s shop window.

This quotation is the other half: getting Google to come and look, getting the shop onto Google Maps, finding the words customers actually type, and then measuring what happens. None of it repeats anything already billed, and nothing already billed is re-charged here.

02

The work

A  ·  Getting found, and getting measured

  • Google Search Console — the site verified against your own domain, the sitemap submitted, and every important page pushed into Google’s queue by hand rather than waiting to be discovered.
  • Indexing checked page by page, so we know which of the 68 pages Google has actually taken and which it has skipped. Today nobody knows, because nothing is connected.
  • Google Analytics connected, so you can see how many people arrive from search, what they look at, and whether any of this is working.
  • Bing and Microsoft Copilot submitted too. Small traffic, but it is free and it is what Copilot answers from.

B  ·  Technical and on-site

  • The rich-result data published and validated against Google’s own testing tool, so your results can carry a price and an in-stock line instead of a bare title.
  • Search and filters on the nine category pages — a customer can narrow by what they want instead of scrolling. This also improves your own shop’s search box.
  • Page speed measured and fixed where it is fixable on this plan — image weight, formats and anything loading that does not need to.
  • The duplicate and empty pages cleaned up, so Google is not given two versions of the same thing to choose between.

C  ·  Google Maps, and the words people type

  • Google Business Profile created and verified — the shop on Google Maps, with the address, both phone numbers, opening hours, photographs and the trading-since-1999 history. For a shop in Horanadu this is probably the single most valuable item on this page, because almost nobody else is competing for those searches.
  • Keyword research on the names that matter — marati moggu, nellikai powder, sikakai, antuvala, chekke, sasive, elakki. Local names where the local name is what gets typed, and English where it does not.
  • Product and category text deepened where it is thin, using those words, without ever adding a claim that is not on the pack.
  • Listed in the free local directories that Google reads to confirm a business is real, with the name, address and phone identical everywhere.

D  ·  At the end of it

  • A written report — what was done, what Google has indexed, which searches you are already appearing for, and the three things worth doing next.
  • Every account in your own name, handed over with the logins. Search Console, Analytics and the Business Profile belong to you, not to me.

E  ·  Not included in this price

Paid Google or Meta advertising and its ad spend · buying links or paid directory placements · writing a blog or an ongoing content calendar · product photography · social media posting · marketplace listings on Amazon or Flipkart · a regional-language version of the shop · any monthly fee after this piece of work ends. Each is available and quoted separately whenever you want it.

03

How long it takes

Two to five weeks. The spread is not padding — two of the steps are held by Google and not by me, and they are the ones that can run long.

Week 1

Connected and measured

Search Console verified, sitemap submitted, Analytics live, Bing submitted, and the Google Business Profile application started. From the end of this week onward, everything is measurable — which it is not today.

Week 2

Technical

Rich-result data published and validated, filters installed on the nine category pages, page speed measured and fixed, duplicate pages cleaned up, indexing checked page by page.

Weeks 3–4

Maps, words and text

Google Business Profile verified and filled in properly. Keyword research done and applied to the product and category text. Local directory listings submitted.

Week 5

Report and handover

The written report, the first real indexing and search data out of Search Console, and all accounts handed to you in your own name.

Why it can reach five weeks. Google Business Profile verification is the usual reason — Google confirms the address by post or by telephone, on its own schedule, and Horanadu is not a quick postal route. Indexing is the other: Google decides when it crawls. Neither is something anyone can speed up, which is why the range is honest rather than flattering.

05

What can be promised, and what cannot

Nobody can promise you a position on Google. Anyone who does is selling something. Google does not sell positions and does not take instructions. What can be promised is that the shop will be technically correct, submitted, measured, on Google Maps, and using the words your customers actually search — which is everything inside anyone’s control.

What to realistically expect

  • Your own name — days to about two weeks. Searching “malnad products” should find you quickly. Nobody competes for your name.
  • Local and specific searches — weeks. “Marati moggu online”, “nellikai powder”, “sikakai powder”, “Malnad spices Horanadu”. This is where the real money is, because the competition is thin and the buyer knows exactly what they want.
  • Broad searches — months, and it is a fight. “Buy almonds online” puts you against national sellers spending lakhs a month. Do not judge this work by those.

One thing that is yours, not mine. Google Business Profile verification needs somebody at the Horanadu address to take a telephone call or receive a postcard, and the Google and Analytics accounts need your own login and your own one-time passwords. I cannot do those for you. Everything else on this page I can.
