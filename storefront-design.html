Our estate

A canopy we did not plant.

Our coffee grows in filtered light, beneath silver oak and native jackwood that were standing long before the first bush went in. Shade slows the cherry down. It ripens over months rather than weeks, and that patience is what you taste.

The monsoon does the rest. Four months of rain, mist that sits in the valley until mid-morning, and red laterite soil that holds the water long after it stops.
