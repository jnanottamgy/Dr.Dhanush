Our shop

A counter in Horanadu.

We grind and pack our own coffee, tea and masala blends under the Swad name, at Devaramane on the Horanadu road. The sambar powder, the puliyogare, the bisibele bath — those are ours, and they are mixed in small lots rather than milled by the tonne.

What we do not make, our neighbours do. A dozen Malnad makers share this counter: ghani-pressed coconut oil from Madanthyar, squashes from Nanjangud, tea from Kalasa. We stock them because people here already buy them.
